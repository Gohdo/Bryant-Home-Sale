Market Drivers & 2026–2027 Outlook
What Shapes This Market
Supply, Demand, Macro Factors

Structural Tailwinds (Long-term Support) University of Washington presence drives consistent rental + buyer demand from graduate students, faculty, families seeking walkability. Tech employment (Amazon, Microsoft, Google offices in Seattle area) anchors professional class. Zero state income tax in Washington = capital efficiency advantage for high earners relocating. Limited buildable land in northeast Seattle maintains supply constraint.

Current Headwinds (2025–2026) Mortgage rates stuck 6.2–6.4% range (not low enough to trigger frenzy, but manageable). Tech sector job cuts (Amazon, Microsoft, Meta layoffs) have cooled hiring momentum. Regional unemployment rising (Seattle lost 12,900 jobs in 2025). Federal uncertainty around immigration & economic policy creates buyer hesitation.

2026–2027 Scenario Planning If rates fall to 5.5–5.8%: expect spring 2027 to mirror 2024–2025 activity surge. DOM collapses to 7–10 days, sale-to-list ratios climb to 105–110%. If rates rise above 7%: market cools further; DOM extends to 30–40 days, sale-to-list falls to 98–99%. Most likely: rates remain mid-sixes, market stays 13–20 day DOM with 101–102% ratios.

Supply Outlook New construction in northeast Seattle has slowed (city is 4 years behind on housing targets). Zoning reforms (HB 1110, density allowances) may unlock more multi-family on single-family lots, but permitting is slow. Net result: 98105 likely remains supply-constrained through 2028+.

Bottom line for sellers in 2026 Moderate seller's market persists. List prices around market value (not inflated). Expect 13–26 day DOM depending on condition/price point. 101–102% sale-to-list likely holds through 2026 if rates stay stable. Spring (Feb–May) strongest selling season; fall/winter slower. Well-priced, well-marketed homes in Bryant still command strong buyer interest.
Practical Takeaways
If You're Selling 5257 36th Ave NE or Nearby
Strategy Recommendations

✓ List in Spring (Feb–May) for maximum buyer exposure and highest sale-to-list premiums. DOM will be 7–13 days in this window.

✓ Price Aggressively (within market range). DOM at 13 days means correctly priced homes sell fast. Overpricing extends DOM to 30+ days, killing momentum.

✓ Stage & Market for multiple-offer potential. With 101–102% sale-to-list ratio, clean homes generate bidding. Professional photos + virtual tour essential.

✓ If Buying in 98105 expect competitive environment. Pre-approval essential. Be ready to move within 24–48 hours on well-priced homes. Negotiate on contingencies (inspection, appraisal) to strengthen offer. Homes selling 101–102% list means limited discounts available.
